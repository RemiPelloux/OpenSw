# Community code of conduct

OpenSw contributors and users must keep project spaces professional, technical and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and evidence-based in reviews, issues and discussions.
- Critique code and decisions without attacking people.
- Respect licenses, copyrights, privacy and responsible disclosure.
- Disclose relevant generated or AI-assisted work and remain accountable for it.
- Help keep reports reproducible and free of games, keys, firmware, saves and personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, piracy assistance and publishing
another person's private information are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments or accounts that violate these rules. Report
conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security advisory channel](https://github.com/RemiPelloux/OpenSw/security/advisories/new). Reports
will be reviewed with discretion and without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
